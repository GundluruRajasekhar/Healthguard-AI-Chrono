# HealthGuard AI Chrono

Open Innovation watchface that combines health monitoring, AI insight and productivity tracking in one circular dashboard.

## Design name
HealthGuard AI Chrono

## Theme / category
Open Innovation

## Concept (for the form)
HealthGuard AI Chrono is an Open Innovation smartwatch watchface designed to combine health monitoring, AI-powered insights, and productivity tracking in a single intuitive interface. The watchface displays time, heart rate, step count, battery level, weather, and activity progress through a clean futuristic layout. Inspired by the growing integration of wearable technology and artificial intelligence, the design promotes healthier lifestyles while maintaining a premium, modern user experience. Vibrant accents, clear typography, and data-driven rings keep it readable throughout the day.

## Target users
Health-conscious professionals, fitness enthusiasts, smartwatch users.

## Innovation highlights
- AI health monitoring with a one-line insight
- Real-time wellness metrics in three activity rings
- Accessible visual hierarchy: time first, data second
- Modern wearable UX built for always-on display
