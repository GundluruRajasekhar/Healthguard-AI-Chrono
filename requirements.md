# Requirements: HealthGuard AI Chrono

Watchface submission for Adobe Creative Cloud, Open Innovation challenge (Hack2skill).

## Goal
A futuristic, AI-powered smartwatch face that shows the time first and health, weather and battery data at a glance.

## Audience
Health-conscious professionals, fitness enthusiasts and everyday smartwatch users.

## Functional requirements
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| F1 | Time (HH:MM) is the largest element |
| F2 | Date and weather sit above the time |
| F3 | Three concentric rings: Move (outer, blue), Steps (middle, green), Heart (inner, cyan) |
| F4 | Numeric BPM, steps and battery values with labels below the time |
| F5 | One-line AI insight under the time |
| F6 | 60 minute ticks, stronger every five minutes |

## Design requirements
| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| D1 | Square 1:1 artboard, circular face centered |
| D2 | Dark background with blue and green health indicators |
| D3 | Style: futuristic, AI-powered, minimalist |
| D4 | Condensed sans-serif type, bold time |
| D5 | Secondary text in a dim slate so the time stays dominant |
| D6 | Thin strokes and small lit areas suit always-on display |

## Submission requirements
- Packaged design folder: zip, up to 5 MB
- Final preview: PNG, aspect ratio 1:1, up to 5 MB
- Design name, theme (Open Innovation) and a 50-100 word concept
- Deadline: 06/12/2026, 11:59 PM IST. Two attempts allowed

## Non-functional requirements
- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 on the dark face
- Each ring is labelled, so colour is not the only cue
- index.html is one self-contained file; animation respects reduced motion

## Out of scope
Live sensor data, watch OS packaging and extra complications.
